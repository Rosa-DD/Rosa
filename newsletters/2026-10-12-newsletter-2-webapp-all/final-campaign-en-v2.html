Why this changes so much

Family life doesn't happen on a phone. Daycare closes early out of nowhere, practice gets cancelled, the dentist moves your appointment, and it all lands on you right when you're at work. With the web app, your family plan sits in a tab right next to your inbox and your work calendar, and in the evening the tablet in the kitchen shows everyone what's coming. Every member of your family gets their own lane there, including the kids who don't have a phone yet.
 
A home screen that celebrates with you 🥳

This is honestly my favourite part. A colourful ring at the top fills up with every task you tick off, and when the day is done, familymind congratulates you properly. Whoever finishes their part gets their own "All done for today" cheer with their name on it, "This week" shows how much you've done together, and your family page counts how many years you've been a family and how many days are left until the next birthday. My son LOVES it, and honestly, so do I 😄
Mia, completely rebuilt 🍝
We've completely rebuilt Mia, our meal planning assistant. She keeps your favourite dishes in your own family cookbook, you add new recipes with a screenshot or a link, and she turns them into your weekly plan and your grocery list. On the big screen you simply drag a dinner onto the right night, the kids get a say, and everyone can see who's cooking when.
Four things to try tonight
1 Bring your partner in. The mental load can only be shared when you both see what's coming up, and familymind even nudges you when someone is still missing. The invite is one click on the family page, or simply forward them this email.
2 Connect your calendar. familymind syncs two ways with Google and Apple Calendar, and Outlook and every other calendar get a calendar link. Work meetings, parents' evenings and doctor's appointments then live in one place, and familymind plans ahead before things get tight.
3 Let Mia plan your week of dinners. Tell her your family's favourite dishes or send a screenshot of a recipe, then plan the week together on the tablet on Sunday.
4 Just ask familymind. "Ask familymind" waits at the bottom of every page. Type whatever's buzzing around your head, like "Who's doing pickup on Thursday?" or "Remind us about the present for Paula's birthday", and familymind adds it and thinks ahead.
