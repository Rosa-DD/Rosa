# Die rote Schaufel — photo picture book prototype

A private photo picture book, a Christmas gift for my children: a flip book (Wendebuch) for ages 2–4. One playground conflict, told from two perspectives, Ben's and Mia's. The images are photoreal but fully fictional, with no photos of real children.
